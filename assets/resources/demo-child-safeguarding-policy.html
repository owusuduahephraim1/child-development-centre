Child Safeguarding Policy (Demo)

This sample policy illustrates how a safeguarding resource may be presented on the website.

Purpose

To demonstrate a child-centred approach to safety, dignity, privacy and responsible conduct across programmes and activities.

Sample principles

Act in the best interests of children; maintain appropriate boundaries; obtain required consent for identifiable media; use confidential reporting channels; and escalate safeguarding concerns promptly.

Important

This is not an adopted or legally reviewed policy. It must be replaced by an approved organisational safeguarding policy.
