Volunteer Guidelines (Demo)

This sample document demonstrates the structure of volunteer guidance for a child-development NGO.

Sample expectations

Respect children and families, follow safeguarding instructions, protect confidential information, work within assigned responsibilities, and report concerns to the designated supervisor.

Conduct

Volunteers should model respectful behaviour, avoid private or inappropriate contact with children, and obtain authorization before taking or sharing photographs.

Important

This demonstration does not replace formal volunteer screening, training, agreements or policies.
